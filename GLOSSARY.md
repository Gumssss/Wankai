# Wankai Game

Terms used to describe the small two-player stick fighting demo.

## Language

**Fighter**:
One of the two upright contestants in a match. Fighters have no feet and move by using their staffs.

**Staff**:
The floating weapon a fighter uses to push against the ground, strike the other fighter, and block attacks.

**Weapon appearance**:
The visible form of a staff. It can change without changing the staff's movement or combat behavior.

**Match**:
A local fight between two players that ends when a fighter's health reaches zero.

**Practice mode**:
A session against a non-attacking target that recovers health after it is no longer being hit.

**Shared view**:
The single camera view of the arena seen by both players in the first demo.

**Player view**:
A camera view centered on one fighter. A future split-view mode would show one player view for each fighter in the same window.
