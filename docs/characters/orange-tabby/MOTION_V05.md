## Asset and controller

Prepared asset v01 preserves the selected Tripo face, markings, seated body, giant paw proportions and clear gaps. It has 25,000 triangles, one original 2K atlas, one material and a root plus two rigid paw controls. Body height is 1.45045 units after reduction. Rest theta is pi/2; virtual pitch pivots are 0.8 above the body base. No clips compete with physics.

W/S uses finite continuous paired paw rotation and reverse braking. A/D steers the common heading toward 1.15 rad/s. Ordinary hovering is an explicit bounded external spring/damper lift (target 0.35, gain 25, damping 9, cap gravity + 12); it is not generated by air swings. Paw gravity is compensated. Floor contacts drive translation and small additional rise. No F ability or global balance change is proposed. Both paws share stamina, attack gates and clash handling.

The current src/physics.js is adapted only in memory. Separate preview loadout normalization allows research profiles without enabling a production character. Actual contact positions and analytical velocities use the same rigid pitch/yaw frame as the exported meshes. A green basic-fighter body and sword capsule are explanatory opponent proxies. No body translation is scripted. The drop test explicitly starts 0.70 above normal hover.

## Measurements and practical limits

Idle/A/D hover is 0.35 units, with zero translation and no idle stamina drain. Ordinary W/S maximum base height is approximately 0.63, compared with about 1.74 in the larger primitive v04 trial. In separate 3-second fixtures W ends at +7.73 X and S at -5.01 X; slight lateral/yaw drift comes from asymmetric contacts. These are review measurements, not accepted balance targets.

The fitted paw spheres stay inside the actual mesh. Maximum uncovered cosmetic surface distance is about 0.14 units (99th percentile about 0.11); there is no inflated invisible hit margin. Ear tips and tail are cosmetic outside the conservative torso/head hurt capsules. The full pitch sweep retains a conservative 0.05996-unit lateral body/paw gap.

Positive hit fixtures start without overlap. Vertical hit first occurs at 1.494 s (2 damage), horizontal at 0.983 s (1 damage, turn contribution 1), and sword clash at 0.461 s. The distant target receives no hit. This demonstrates contact functions; hit strength and pace remain a trial. Contact-frame finite-difference velocity error is 0.00000916 units/s.

Installed Three.js GLTFLoader checks pass for hierarchy, budget, full-turn geometry/contact agreement (max sampled error 0.000000024), independent player control/material state, all ordinary inputs, coasting and original source preservation. Browser texture/input/reset checks and encoded-video verification are recorded in the versioned verification files.

A two-cat Node solver smoke run also completes in less than simulated real time. The 48-sphere fit costs more than simple weapons; see solver-cost-smoke.json for the concurrent-render measurement. This does not establish browser frame rate. Profile the actual integrated two-player game before playable acceptance.
