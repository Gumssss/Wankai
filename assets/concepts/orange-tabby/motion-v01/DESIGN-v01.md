# 橘猫·猫爪 / Orange tabby paw fighter

19 October 2026. Provisional direction v01; new additional playable loadout requested. The two supplied images are selected references. Posture, dimensions, joints and controls below are recommendations awaiting motion review. No production model, ability or cat spending is approved.

## Identity and appearance locks

Use one rounded orange tabby from the supplied collage, with a broad cheeked head, triangular ears, expressive green eyes, small pink nose, white muzzle/bib and white paws. Preserve orange forehead/cheek/back stripes and a ringed tail. Treat the pictured poses as the same identity. Exclude screenshot UI, paper background and extra cats.

The second reference supplies broad white paw volumes, four pink toe pads plus a central pad, tiny pointed claws and a striped orange wrist. Both weapons are the cat's attached front paws; no floating gloves or handheld weapons. Small claws communicate contact, while the paw volume supplies support and block coverage. Keep smooth matte volume rather than individual fur strands. Flat black source outlines are an illustration cue, not a required 3D contour.

At Wankai's split-screen distance, the first reads are triangular ears, round cheeks/body, two white working paws and a curved striped tail. The second reads are orange/white contrast and darker stripes. Pads/claws are close-view details; do not rely on their visibility for input feedback. Player identification can use the existing HUD/rim marker before changing the selected fur palette.

## Recommended posture and alternative

**A — compact seated scoot (recommended):** rounded haunches near the floor, head upright, hind feet tucked. The front limbs do the work. Two shoulder–elbow chains extend the paws into a low raking stroke, then fold to recover outside the torso. Alternate contacts avoid making two planted paws an impossible rigid steering lock. Tail follows cosmetically and is not a weapon. No independent walking or hind-leg propulsion.

**B — taller haunch-supported stance:** same identity and paw chain lengths, but higher shoulders. It makes the pad-facing guard clearer while reducing support depth. The comparison video will use the same scale and paw reach. This is a posture comparison, not a settled extra moveset.

The concept blockout simplifies the face, stripes and volumes; it is not an appearance candidate or a substitute for the later Tripo base.

## Game context and provisional geometry

Electron/React Three Fiber/Three.js, current custom upright contact solver, local keyboard co-op. Read current local source, including accepted beagle normal movement and timed hover; never reset to the earlier merged checkpoint. Cat integration is additive after asset/motion reviews.

Author +X forward, +Z up in Blender; export GLB with +Y up. One authoring unit equals one game unit. The current blockout has a roughly 2.3-unit ear-tip height and 1.15-unit torso width, chosen for this cat rather than copied from beagle. Candidate shoulder heights: A 1.02, B 1.37 above the root's floor reference. Upper/lower front limb lengths: 0.62 / 0.66; paw support radius 0.20. Shoulder half-spacing 0.65 clears the round torso. These are blockout experiment dimensions, not production locks.

Provisional asset targets: about 25k triangles or fewer, one 2K atlas or smaller, at most three materials, at most four influences per vertex and approximately 4 MB GLB. Actual source measurements and visual losses will determine revisions. No fixed bone count is selected before motion proof.

## Ordinary actions and ability boundary

Propose W/S as opposite directions of a continuous paw stroke cycle, with reverse braking. A/D steers the common paw plane and the cat's heading, with finite drive; it does not spread the paws into a rotor. W/S+A/D combines rake and steering. Paired paws share ordinary stamina and repeat-hit rules; two paws do not imply double cost or damage. The existing game defaults remain the integration baseline.

No special ability is proposed for acceptance in v01. Do not inherit beagle F, hover, flips, duration or resource decisions. First judge whether short anatomical paws can support useful ordinary travel and combat.
