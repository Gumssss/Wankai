## Ordinary actions and ability boundary

Propose W/S as opposite directions of synchronized continuous paw swings, with reverse braking; elbows connect the outboard paw plane to the shoulders. A/D steers the common paw plane and the cat's heading, with finite drive; it does not spread the paws into a rotor. W/S+A/D combines rake and steering. Paired paws share ordinary stamina and repeat-hit rules; two paws do not imply double cost or damage. The existing game defaults remain the integration baseline.

No special ability is proposed for acceptance in v02. Do not inherit beagle F, hover, flips, duration or resource decisions. First judge the giant-paw leverage, clearance, support and combat coverage.
